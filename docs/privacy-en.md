# MyHandMemo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

## About the app

MyHandMemo is a handwritten notepad you write on with your finger, directly on the iPhone screen.

There is no keyboard — you scribble the way you would on paper. Switch between pen, marker, and eraser, pick an ink color (black, blue, red, or yellow) and one of three stroke widths. Paper can be plain, ruled, or grid. Notes are laid out as thumbnails, and each one can be pinned, duplicated, shared as an image, or deleted. The app's display language can be set to Match Device, Japanese, or English.

No account is required. **The app has no networking, so every feature works without an internet connection.**

## Personal information we collect

**None.**

This app collects no personal information whatsoever. It does not obtain your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photo library contents, or any other personal data. It never asks you to create an account or sign in.

## What is stored on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone. No one, including the developer, can see it.

| Stored item | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Note strokes | The drawing data for the lines you write |
| Note thumbnails | Images used to show notes in the list |
| Note timestamps and pin state | Information used for ordering the list |
| App settings | Display language, default paper, default ink color and stroke width |

All of this lives in the app's own storage on the device and is never transmitted to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data along with it.

## Network communication

The app performs no network communication. It contains no networking code, connects to no server, and embeds no third-party libraries.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

- No analytics tools are used
- No advertisements are shown
- Users are not tracked
- No data is shared with third parties

## Backup and sync

If iCloud Backup is enabled in your iPhone settings, this app's data may be included in Apple's iCloud backup. This is a standard iOS behavior and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to the contents of that backup.

## About sharing

Pressing and holding a note and choosing "Share as Image" opens the standard iOS share sheet. You decide, each time, whether to share and where to send it (Mail, Messages, Photos, and so on). Nothing leaves the device unless you take that action. Once shared, the data is handled according to the privacy policy of the receiving app or service.

## Children

This app is not directed at any particular age group, and because it collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children under 13 either.
